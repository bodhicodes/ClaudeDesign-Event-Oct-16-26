[Org name] Brand system · v1.0

Warm hands. Clear water.

One system for the website, the donor report, the field poster and the feed. Built to feel human first, and hopeful because it is specific.

Principle 1

Warm, never pitying

People appear as capable neighbours and experts, not as victims.

Principle 2

Hopeful, with proof

Optimism is earned by naming what changed: a tap, a well, a school day regained.

Principle 3

Plain and sturdy

Works on a printed field poster, a phone screen and a funder deck alike.

Colour

Sun on water, earth underfoot

Teal carries trust. Orange and amber carry the warmth and the hope. Amber 50 keeps everything human instead of clinical. Every colour is from Tailwind's default palette.
