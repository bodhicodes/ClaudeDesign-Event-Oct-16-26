A rising line with a yearly rhythm.

Each point is one monthly mean at Mauna Loa. Plants take up CO2 in the northern summer, so readings peak in May and fall to a low in September or October. The climb underneath keeps going.

Annual growth rate

Growth slowed in 2025, but CO2 still rose.

The annual growth rate is the change in concentration from January to December. After two years above 3.3 ppm, 2025 came in at 2.23 ppm.

Read the numbers with care

The last year of data is preliminary, pending recalibration of reference gases and other quality checks. After the 2022 eruption, measurements moved to a nearby site. Readings from December 2022 to 4 July 2023 come from the Maunakea Observatories. NOAA last updated its page on 9 October 2026.

The full NOAA page is at gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends. It holds the monthly, daily and weekly series and the data files.
